Lifelixir Clinic Manager

Patient records, visits, prescriptions and appointments for medical clinics.

Lifelixir Clinic Manager is an application for doctors and their clinic staff. It keeps patient files, visits, prescriptions, lab results, appointments and the clinic's finances, and works on Windows, Android and in the browser.

Google sign-in

A clinic can choose to keep its records in its own Google account. When the clinic signs in with Google, the app creates a spreadsheet in that clinic's Google Drive and works only with the files it created there. The developer never receives a copy of these records.
