Privacy Policy

Lifelixir Clinic Manager · last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains what Lifelixir Clinic Manager ("the app") does with information, in particular the information it receives when a clinic signs in with a Google account.

Who we are

The app is developed by Dr. Zaid J Alhussainy & Dr. Ahmed Almosawe, Baghdad, Iraq. Contact: zaidjk01@gmail.com.

Information the app uses from Google

When a clinic chooses "Sign in with Google", the app asks for:

  • Your name, e-mail address and profile picture (openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile), used only to show which Google account the clinic is connected to and to let the clinic sign in again on its other devices.
  • Access to files the app creates in your Google Drive (drive.file). The app creates the clinic's spreadsheet there and reads and writes only that spreadsheet and the files the app itself made. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Where the clinic's records are kept

Patient files, visits and the other clinic records are kept on the clinic's own devices and in the spreadsheet in the clinic's own Google account. They go directly from the clinic's device to Google. The developer does not receive, store or have access to them.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train artificial-intelligence models.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties, except as needed to provide the app's features the clinic uses, to comply with the law, or with the clinic's permission.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services the clinic may switch on

Some optional features (for example online bookings or sending a prescription to a patient's phone) work through a separate service that the clinic connects in Settings. Those features send only what is needed for them (such as a booking or a prescription) and are not part of the Google sign-in.

Security

Communication with Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access inside the clinic is protected with personal PIN codes for each staff member.

Removing access and deleting data

The clinic can disconnect the app at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The clinic's spreadsheet stays in its own Google Drive, where the clinic can keep or delete it. For any question or request, write to zaidjk01@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
